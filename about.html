About Us

About The Color Code: who we are and how to reach us

The Color Code is a free personal color analysis that finds your color season, palette and makeup shades in about a minute. Five Tulane University students in New Orleans built it and run it.

We do our own makeup daily, live color theory in our everyday choices, and are part of the sororities and clubs at Tulane that we built this for.

Where we are and how to reach us

Where we are

Tulane University, New Orleans, Louisiana, United States. The analysis itself runs on your phone, wherever you are.

How to reach us

Message us on Instagram at @thecolorcode.collective. That is also how to book a chapter color night. We post on TikTok at @thecolorcode_.
