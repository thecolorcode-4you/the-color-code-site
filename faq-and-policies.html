What happens to my photos?

Your selfie is optional. If you take one, it's used once, only to read your skin, eye and hair color, and deleted as soon as your result is back. We never save it, and no one on our team ever sees it. Rather not share a photo? Skip it — your six answers alone still give you a season.

Who actually does my analysis?

Bella is the name of our AI color-analysis agent — we gave her a name to make the experience feel more personal, but she's not a person reviewing your submission by hand. Every analysis runs through the same automated method, applied consistently to every submission, which is also what makes instant results possible.

How long does it take to get my results?

About a minute: six quick questions, then your result. With a selfie, Bella takes about 15 seconds to read your photo. Your palette appears right in the app. It stays on your phone, and it's saved to your account if you have one.

Can I ask for a redo if my result looks off?

Yes — retake it any time, free. For the best read, take a new selfie facing a window, bare-faced or in light makeup; lighting and foundation are the most common reasons a photo reads differently. You can also skip the photo and answer the questions alone. If it still looks off, message us on Instagram at @thecolorcode.collective and we'll take a look.
