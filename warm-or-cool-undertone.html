How do I know if I have warm or cool undertones?

You can tell whether your undertone is warm or cool from four everyday signs: the color of the veins on your inner wrist, whether gold or silver jewelry flatters you more, whether you look better in bright white or soft ivory, and how your skin reacts to the sun. Green veins, gold jewelry, ivory and tanning easily point to a warm undertone; blue or purple veins, silver jewelry, bright white and burning easily point to a cool one. If your answers split, you are close to neutral, and your hair, eyes and how light or deep your coloring is decide which side you lean to. These are the same four signs our free quiz asks about, and it weighs them for you in about a minute.

The four signs, and how our quiz counts each one

Most undertone tests online give you one sign and a verdict. Our quiz asks all four, and the season rules we wrote give each one a set weight, because no single sign is reliable on its own.

1. Your veins

Look at the inside of your wrist in daylight. Blue or purple veins count one point toward cool. Green veins count one point toward warm. A mix of both counts as neutral. If you honestly can't tell, choose that: we leave your veins out rather than guess.

2. Gold or silver

Think about which jewelry people compliment on you. Silver counts one point toward cool, gold one point toward warm, and looking good in both counts as neutral.

3. Bright white or soft ivory

Hold a bright white shirt and a cream or ivory one under your chin. Bright white counts one point toward cool; ivory counts one point toward warm. Bright white also counts toward "clear" coloring, which matters for the Bright seasons.

4. The sun

Burning easily leans cool and tanning easily leans warm, but each counts only half a point, because sun reaction is more about how light or deep your skin is. If your skin rarely burns and just gets deeper, we count that toward depth only, never toward warm or cool, so it can't push you either way by itself.

When your signs disagree

Split answers are common, and they don't mean the test failed. Our rules add your points up. Hair adds a light hint of half a point (golden and red hair lean warm, ash and black lean cool), but no more than that, because red or golden hair can belong to a cool Winter. If your total lands exactly on neutral, golden or red hair tips you warm; otherwise you lean cool.

Undertone is also only one of three things that decide your colors. The other two are depth (how light or deep your hair, eyes and skin are) and clarity (whether bold, saturated colors or soft, dusty ones suit you). Checking your undertone with a selfie

If you add a selfie, Bella, our AI color analyst, reads your skin undertone from the photo in about 15 seconds. When she is sure, the photo counts as much as two of your answers; when she is unsure, it counts as one. Photos taken indoors or in mixed light count for half, because yellow lamps make everyone look warmer. For the best read, face a window, bare-faced or in light makeup. The selfie is optional, it is deleted as soon as your result is back, and no one on our team ever sees it.
