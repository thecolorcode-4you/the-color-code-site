Why This Matters

Why it helps to know your colors

"People guess at what colors suit them — and waste money on the wrong ones."

The Color Code tells you, scientifically, which colors actually look good on you — no more guessing. (From our venture pitch.)

Most people guess at what colors suit them — and end up spending on the wrong ones. The Color Code replaces that guesswork with a palette that's specific down to the shade, plus the exact makeup tones to match it, built on real analysis of your skin undertone, eye color, and hair color rather than a generic quiz. And your photos stay private the whole way through — used only to generate your analysis, never sold, shared, or made public.
