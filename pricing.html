Pricing

The Color Code pricing: free during our Tulane pilot

The Color Code costs nothing during our Tulane pilot. Your personal color analysis is free, with no card and nothing to pay at any step, and a chapter's first color night is free in exchange for posting your results and tagging us. We have not set prices for after the pilot.

What each option costs

Personal color analysis

$0

For anyone. Seven questions and an optional selfie give you your season, 8 colors to wear, 3 to skip, and 2 lip, 2 blush and 3 eye shades, saved to your free account. Retakes are free too.

Start your free analysis →

Chapter color night

$0 for your first

For Tulane sorority chapters. Every member finds her season on her own phone. Your first color night is free in exchange for posting your results and tagging us. Book by messaging us on Instagram at @thecolorcode.collective with your chapter, date and roughly how many members.
