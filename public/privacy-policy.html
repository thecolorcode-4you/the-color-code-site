Privacy Policy

Your photo is yours.

The short version

  • We use your selfie and answers only to create your color analysis.
  • We never store your photo. It's sent to our AI provider for your analysis, and that's it.
  • We never sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • Research use is a separate, optional choice. Saying no never affects your result.
  • You can ask us to delete everything at any time.

What we collect

  • Your selfie: used for your analysis only, and never stored by us (see "Who can see it").
  • Your quiz answers and result: your six answers, your season, a short description of your coloring (such as "warm undertone, medium depth"), and the consent choices you made.
  • If you create a free account: your name, your email address and a password. We store your password only in scrambled (hashed) form, so no one on our team can see it.
  • Basic technical data: like any website, our hosting provider automatically handles technical details such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure.

We keep your result (never the photo) even if you don't create an account, without your name or email, so you can save it to an account afterwards. Your phone also keeps a copy so you can come back to it.

We use one cookie, which keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising cookies.

How we use it

  • To work out your season and show you your palette.
  • To run your account, including signing you in, keeping your saved palettes and contacting you about your analysis.
  • Only if you opt in: to use anonymized answers and results (with your name and email removed, and never your photo) to improve our method and for research.

Who can see it

  • You. You can see your saved palettes when you're signed in.
  • Our team. The five members of The Color Code team may view saved results, only to check and improve our analysis and to help you.
  • Anthropic (our AI provider). Your selfie and answers are sent to Claude, Anthropic's AI, which reads your coloring and suggests your season. Anthropic handles them under its commercial terms, which don't allow training its models on them by default, and may keep them for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring.
  • Cloudflare (hosting). Our website and database run on Cloudflare, which stores the data on our behalf and isn't allowed to use it for anything else. Your photo passes through our site on its way to Anthropic but is never saved.

Reviews are separate: a review appears on our site only if you submit one, give separate consent, and our team approves it.

How long we keep it

We never keep your photo. We keep your account, results and saved palettes until you ask us to delete them, so you can come back to your colors any time.

Your choices

  • See your data: sign in and open My Analysis.
  • Delete your data: email us and we'll delete your account, answers and results within 30 days.
  • Withdraw research consent: email us at any time. Anonymized data that has already been used in research can't be pulled back.

Keeping it safe

Your saved results can only be viewed by you, when signed in, and by our team. All traffic to our site is encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but if we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we'll tell you promptly.

Age

The Color Code is for people who are 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll ask for your permission first.
