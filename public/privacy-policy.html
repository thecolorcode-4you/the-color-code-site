The short version

  • We use your photo and answers only to create your color analysis report.
  • We never sell your information, never share it for advertising, and never post your photo publicly.
  • Research use is a separate, optional choice. Saying no never affects your report.
  • You can ask us to delete everything at any time.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, your email address and a password. We store your password only in scrambled (hashed) form, so no one on our team can see it.
  • Your analysis: the photo you upload, your questionnaire answers, and the consent choices you made.
  • Your report: the season and palette we create for you.
  • Basic technical data: like any website, our hosting provider automatically handles technical details such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the site and keep it secure.

We use one cookie, which keeps you signed in. We don't use advertising cookies.

How we use it

  • To create, store and show you your color analysis report.
  • To run your account, including signing you in and contacting you about your analysis.
  • Only if you opt in: to use anonymized data (with your name, email and photo removed) to improve our method and for research.

Who can see it

  • You. You can see your own analysis when you're signed in.
  • Our team. The five members of The Color Code team may view submissions, only to create and check reports.
  • Service providers. Our website, database and photo storage run on Cloudflare, which stores the data on our behalf and isn't allowed to use it for anything else.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and analysis until you ask us to delete them, so you can come back to your palette any time.

Your choices

  • See your data: sign in and open My Analysis.
  • Delete your data: email us and we'll delete your account, photo, answers and report within 30 days.
  • Withdraw research consent: email us at any time. Anonymized data that has already been used in research can't be pulled back.

Keeping it safe

Photos are stored privately and can only be viewed by you, when signed in, and by our team. All traffic to our site is encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but if we ever learn of a breach affecting your data, we'll tell you promptly.

Age

The Color Code is for people who are 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use photos you've already given us, we'll ask for your permission first.
