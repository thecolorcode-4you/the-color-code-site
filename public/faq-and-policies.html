FAQ & Policies

Questions, answered.

Is this free?

Yes — this first pilot at Tulane is completely free (Decision 2).

What happens to my photos?

They're used only to create your color analysis report. They're never sold, shared or made public, and only you and our team can see them. Letting us use anonymized data for research is a separate, optional choice that never affects your report. Read the full privacy policy.

(The privacy policy is a draft awaiting review by Whitney, Bella & Sofia.)

Who actually does my analysis?

Bella is the name of our AI color-analysis agent — we gave her a name to make the experience feel more personal, but she's not a person reviewing your submission by hand. Every analysis runs through the same automated method, applied consistently to every submission, which is also what makes instant results possible.

How long does it take to get my results?

It's instant — the moment you submit, your full report is ready. View it on the site, download it, or have it emailed to you.

Can I ask for a redo if my result looks off?

Yes — flagging a submission for re-analysis if a result looks inconsistent is supported (per consistency-checker in PREPARE-WEBSITE.md).
