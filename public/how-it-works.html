The Method

What "Analyze" actually means

Your features, decoded

Every analysis starts with four inputs: skin undertone (warm, cool, or neutral), skin depth, eye color, and hair color. We read how these interact — not any one in isolation — because your undertone changes which reds read warm on you versus cool, and your depth determines whether you come alive in muted tones or saturated ones. That combination places you in one of twelve seasonal palettes, each with its own hue, value, and chroma range.

Color theory, applied

Seasonal color analysis is built on color theory fundamentals — hue (the color family), value (how light or dark), and chroma (how saturated or muted). Your natural coloring already sits somewhere on each of those scales. We match your palette to shades that share your hue temperature, value range, and saturation, so instead of guessing at a favorite color, you get a full range engineered to harmonize with your specific features.

Why it's worth knowing

Once you know your palette, it applies everywhere — clothes, makeup, hair color, even how you accessorize. Shopping gets faster because you can rule out what won't work before you try it on. Outfits pull together more easily because everything in your palette is already built to complement everything else. And the colors you wear actively work for you — brightening your complexion, matching your eyes, evening out your undertone — instead of leaving it to chance.

Getting your results

Your analysis is instant — no waiting on a human's schedule. The moment you submit, Bella's engine reads your features and builds your palette right away. From there, you can view your full report on the site, download it, or have it emailed to you. Your report doesn't just hand you a palette — it walks through the color theory behind it, so you understand why each shade works for you.
