For chapters

Chapter color nights for Tulane sororities

A sisterhood event where every member finds her season on her own phone, and your chapter sees who shares a season — perfect before formal and bid day photos. The selfie is optional for every member, and no photo is ever saved.

Pilot price: free. During the Tulane pilot, your chapter's first color night is free in exchange for posting your results and tagging us.

Book it: message us on Instagram at @thecolorcode.collective with your chapter, your date and roughly how many members. Joely and Sofia, who work with Panhellenic, will set it up with you.

Running it: share the app link in your chapter GroupMe; each member takes the quiz in about a minute and posts her season. The chapter season spread below — collected for you automatically — is coming later.
